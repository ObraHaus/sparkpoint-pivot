### Fixed (comparative follow-up — 067, 2026-10-29)

- **A grounded “which is safer” comparison is now served instead of replaced.** After 066 both sides of *“Is Morpho safer than Aave? Compare them for a cautious user.”* were grounded — beta's fallback even said *“SparkPoint has verified material on both”* — but the turn still returned the code-authored fallback, uncited, because 066 treated any **declining opening** (“I can't say which is safer…”) as a non-answer: a substantive answer had to be ≥220 characters **and** contain one of 12 literal phrases, and `_answer_can_cite` rejected every decline opening outright. Classification is now by **substance**: clauses that actually describe the named sides. A declining-opening answer that compares them is served and cited; a pure decline/deflection is still replaced. (`coordinator/dag.py`)
- **Comparative outcomes are observable without logging content.** Each comparative prose outcome now emits `comparative_outcome subjects=%d covered=%d reason=%s cited=%s` (`served`, `guard_meta`, `guard_action`, `refused`, `empty`, `non_answer`, `decline`, `upstream`). It contains only counts and enums — never the user message, model text, wallet or citation endpoint — so the next replacement can be diagnosed from the app log instead of guessed from the response. (`coordinator/dag.py`)
- **OH Defender follow-ups from #330/#331.** Whole-message retrieval hits that genuinely name a protocol now count as coverage (the hint/fallback can no longer falsely say “no verified material”); deictic turns (“compare them”) with no extracted subjects keep 065's citation behaviour; the new META verb patterns match curly apostrophes (`doesn’t`/`don’t`/`didn’t`); the proper-noun fallback no longer treats `AND/OR/NOT/VS` or `Does/Should/Would/Can/Has/When/Which/What/Is/Are/How` as protocols. The coverage hint and `COMPARATIVE_RULE` also say explicitly: if the grounded material cannot rank the options, say so once and **still compare the risk factors** rather than stopping at the refusal. (`coordinator/dag.py`, `coordinator/comparative.py`, `agents/guard_text.py`, `agents/sparky.py`)
- **Regression tests:** a substantive declining-opening comparison is served and cited (streaming on and off, long and 2–4-sentence variants), a pure decline is still replaced and uncited, whole-message-only coverage cites both, a deictic comparison cites, the outcome log carries no content, curly-apostrophe scaffolding is caught, and the extractor drops grammar words. Run against `origin/main` (066) the new end-to-end cases fail **14/16**; on 067 the focused suite is green. The full agent suite is **2150 passed, 12 failed** — all 12 are the pre-existing `test_vault_reader` forge-artifact failures (`SparkPointVault.json` not built), reported not fixed. (`tests/coordinator/test_comparative_questions.py`, `tests/agents/test_guard_text.py`)

### Fixed (grounded comparative answers — 066, 2026-10-29)

- **Comparative questions now retrieve material for each named protocol.** 065 stopped the fake `UPSTREAM_UNAVAILABLE`, but a comparative turn still embedded the whole sentence once (`k=3`, `min_score=0.35`). On beta the top hits for *"Is Morpho safer than Aave? Compare them for a cautious user."* were two release-note changelog entries, so the model never saw the protocol notes and deflected: *"I'm not able to compare Morpho and Aave because the reference information doesn't include details…"* — **and a citation block was attached to that non-answer**. The coordinator now extracts the named subjects (`coordinator/comparative.py`) and retrieves the LEARN-route unthresholded top-k **per subject**, merges those with the whole-message hits (filtered to passages that actually name a subject), dedupes and caps the prompt. A cosine hit that never names the protocol is not coverage. (`coordinator/comparative.py`, `coordinator/dag.py`)
- **Partial or missing coverage is still honest and useful.** The prose turn receives a coverage instruction: compare the grounded side, say plainly that SparkPoint has no verified material for the other side, and give only well-known structural differences with no figures. The 065 `_COMPARATIVE_FALLBACK` is now a genuine last resort (content rejection or a pure decline), names the actual extracted subjects instead of “those options”, and names the ungrounded side. Action-verb comparative turns, the 063 history-aware follow-up query and the 065 provider-error mapping are unchanged. (`coordinator/dag.py`, `agents/sparky.py`)
- **The META guard catches scaffolding deflections precisely.** New patterns cover “reference information”, “reference material I can draw from”, “the information provided/available to me”, “the context/documents/passages/material (don’t) include/cover/mention” and “my sources”. Ordinary answers that mention “reference rate” or “documentation on the Vault page” still pass (pinned both ways). (`agents/guard_text.py`)
- **Citations only on real, grounded answers.** A citation block now requires a model-produced answer that passed the guard, is not a deflection or fallback, and names every subject that was actually grounded. `_DECLINE_RE` recognises curly apostrophes (`I’m`), which is why the beta deflection had been treated as a citable answer. (`coordinator/dag.py`)
- **Corpus coverage for the compared protocols.** `knowledge_seed/protocol-basics.md` adds conservative, non-numeric structural notes for Aave, Compound, Morpho, Lido and Rocket Pool (no APY/TVL figures; each explicitly not insured). The seed is **not** ingested at boot — only the changelog is — so beta needs one documented `python -m sparkpoint_agent.knowledge.ingest_cli` (or admin `POST /admin/knowledge/reingest`) after deploy; nothing was run against beta in this branch. (`services/agent/knowledge_seed/protocol-basics.md`, `specs/066-comparative-grounding/plan.md`)
- **Regression tests:** the three beta cases are replayed offline with a fake LLM and fake retriever, streaming **on and off** — both sides grounded retrieves both notes and cites both, one-sided coverage names and cites only the grounded side, zero coverage is useful and uncited, the deflection falls back to the real subject names, and a pure curly-apostrophe decline is never cited. (`tests/coordinator/test_comparative_questions.py`, `tests/agents/test_guard_text.py`, `tests/knowledge/test_knowledge_seed_content.py`)

### Fixed (comparative answers + agent observability — 065, 2026-10-29)

- **Comparative questions no longer fail with `UPSTREAM_UNAVAILABLE`.** On beta, `"Is Morpho safer than Aave? Compare them for a cautious user."` reliably ended in an error block after ~18s (streaming) / ~45s (non-streaming). The model had answered: it produced the honest deflection *"I don't have details on Morpho or Aave safety in my reference notes, so I can't compare…"*. That text trips the shared META guard (`\breference notes?\b` in `agents/guard_text.py`) — correctly, because it narrates the model's own scaffolding — but the coordinator collapsed every `fell_back` (content rejection and provider exception alike) into `_GENERAL_FAILURE` → `ErrorBlock("UPSTREAM_UNAVAILABLE")`, telling the user the model was unreachable when it had just replied. `SparkyReply` now carries a `failure` kind (`upstream` vs `meta`/`action`/`refused`/`empty`), the DAG only surfaces `UPSTREAM_UNAVAILABLE` for a true provider exception, and a comparative turn whose answer was content-rejected gets a code-authored honest comparison (risk, curator, liquidity, yield/fee structure; no invented figures, no advice) instead of a fake outage. A deterministic content rejection is no longer retried on the comparative branch, so the turn no longer pays for a second identical model call. The 063/059 opening guard is unchanged (a legitimate comparison still passes; action-shaped output is still discarded), and action-verb comparative turns still take the planner path. (`agents/guard_text.py`, `agents/sparky.py`, `coordinator/dag.py`)
- **The agent now emits its INFO logs in production.** `sparkpoint_agent.*` had no handler and inherited the root `WARNING` level, so retry/refund/indexer/quota INFO lines were silently dropped — which is why this incident had no app-side trail. `configure_logging()` runs at app startup, honours `LOG_LEVEL` (default `INFO`), attaches one handler to the `sparkpoint_agent` tree and does not propagate to uvicorn/root (no duplicate lines). Existing INFO calls were reviewed for wallet leakage; the three admin self-authoring lines that logged `admin.owner` are now address-free (the audit table still records the actor). (`logging_config.py`, `api/main.py`, `api/admin_self_authoring.py`)
- **Regression tests:** the beta deflection is replayed with a fake LLM for streaming on AND off — no error block, an honest comparison naming Morpho and Aave, no second prose call; a true provider exception still yields `UPSTREAM_UNAVAILABLE`; guard classification is pinned both ways; and a test asserts `sparkpoint_agent.api.quota` INFO records are emitted by the configured logger. (`tests/coordinator/test_comparative_questions.py`, `tests/agents/test_guard_text.py`, `tests/agents/test_sparky.py`, `tests/test_logging_config.py`). `MIGRATIONS.md` now records beta at `0043` (applied 2026-10-28).

### Security — mint-whitelist CSV export (2026-10-29)

- **Spreadsheet formulas in the admin mint-whitelist export are neutralized.** `name`, `contact` and `reason` come from an unauthenticated public form, so a value beginning with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, TAB or CR (including after leading whitespace) could execute as a formula when an operator opened the downloaded CSV. ### Fixed (BFF null-body passthrough — 064)

- **Thumbs feedback no longer reports a 500.** `POST /chat/feedback` stores the rating and correctly answers `204 No Content`, but the BFF rebuilt that response as `new Response(arrayBuffer, { status: 204 })`. The Fetch `Response` constructor rejects any body for a null-body status (`TypeError: Invalid response status code 204`), so the route threw after the write had succeeded and the browser saw a 500. The proxy now returns `new Response(null, { status, headers })` for every null-body status (101, 103, 204, 205, 304), dropping `content-length` (and `content-type` for 204/205); all other JSON responses and the `/chat` SSE stream are unchanged. `AgentClient.request` already returned `undefined` for 204/205 without parsing and is now pinned by a test. (`apps/web/src/lib/agent/proxyResponse.ts`, `apps/web/src/app/api/agent/[...path]/route.ts`)

### Fixed (true streaming + comparative answers, 063)

- **Sparky now streams tokens all the way to the browser.** The agent was already framing `event: text-delta` records, but the in-container LiteLLM guard proxy (`services/litellm/guard.py`) proxied `POST /chat/completions` with `httpx.request(...)` and rebuilt the reply from `upstream.content`, so a `stream=true` completion was buffered until the model finished. The guard now detects a streaming request, uses `send(stream=True)` and relays the upstream body chunk-by-chunk through `StreamingResponse` (raw bytes, hop-by-hop/content-length headers stripped, connection closed in `finally`); non-streaming JSON keeps the buffered path. The prose agents keep a 40-character opening hold: `Sparky.stream_reply` / `Literacy.stream_tip` buffer the opening until it reaches 40 characters (or the stream ends), then run the shared action/meta prefix guard and paint the held text as one delta before streaming every later chunk live. The final guarded `text` / `tip` block is still authoritative (refusal, `is_unusable`, `MAX_CHARS` clamp, retry with a new `streamId`, static fallback), so a clamp/rewrite/discard still replaces what was painted. (`services/litellm/guard.py`, `agents/sparky.py`, `agents/literacy.py`, `agents/guard_text.py`) Measured locally with a 5-delta mock model: first `text-delta` fell from ~100 % of the turn (at `done`) to **~28 %** for a general question and **~25 %** for a tip; the deterministic price path is a ~20 ms complete block with no deltas. (`specs/063-true-streaming/measurements.md`)
- **The opening guard keeps a 40-character hold while streaming.** The first 063 cut released on the first chunk, so a token-sized prefix like “We” passed and a later meta/action shape in the opening painted unguarded; the hold is measured in characters (or the stream end), never chunks, and an unusable window paints nothing while the terminal guarded block stays authoritative. (Defender MEDIUM)
- **Comparative questions get a real, grounded comparison instead of canned yield copy.** “Is Morpho safer than Aave?”, “Morpho vs Aave — which is safer?”, “Which is safer, Morpho or Aave?” and “Compare that with just keeping it in Aave.” now route to the grounded Sparky prose branch: no planner, no yield/mandate composer, no curated-board pick, and never an action/recommendation. The comparison turn receives the retrieved protocol facts plus a code-authored COMPARISON rule (risk, yield mechanics, fees/structure, no invented figures, no advice, no winner); a deictic follow-up also receives the recent conversation (fenced as untrusted data) and retrieval runs over those turns so “that” resolves. A comparative turn that is also an explicit command (“compare them, then deposit 500 USDC”) keeps the normal planner/action path. The old empty-wallet answer began “there's nothing for a mandate to apply to”, which is not an answer to a risk comparison. (`coordinator/guidance.py`, `coordinator/dag.py`, `agents/sparky.py`)
- **Regression coverage:** a real-uvicorn `/chat` + middleware test asserts the first `text-delta` arrives in under 40 % of the turn; a guard-proxy test asserts chunk-by-chunk SSE relay; comparative routing tests cover both named prompts, variants, history and the explicit-command guard; the mocked Playwright chat-streaming spec now asserts a PARTIAL paint before the final block and that the full answer lands later. (`tests/api/test_chat_streaming_progressive.py`, `tests/api/test_gateway_guard_streaming.py`, `tests/coordinator/test_comparative_questions.py`, `apps/web/e2e/pw/chat-streaming.spec.ts`)

### Fixed (quota refund audit, 062)

- **A refunded turn no longer logs a false `quota_refund_failed` alarm.** `quota.refund()` returned the counter in its own autocommitted `UPDATE usage_counters … GREATEST(count - 1, 0)` and only then wrote a `limits_event` row with verdict `refund` — but `migrations/0015_limits.sql` permits only `allow`/`deny`/`throttle`, so that INSERT always raised a CheckViolation which the bare `except` logged as a refund failure. The counter refund had already committed, so users were refunded, but no refund was audited and a genuine refund failure was indistinguishable. `refund()` now logs `quota_refunded metric=… used=…` on success and `quota_refund_failed metric=… err=<ExceptionClassName>` on failure (class name only — no message, no address), and writes **no** `limits_event` row; the structured log is the audit trail. `db/limits.py` exports `EVENT_VERDICTS` mirroring the 0015 CHECK and `record_event()` raises `ValueError` for any other verdict **before** touching the DB, so a future misuse fails loudly in tests. No migration; if DB-level refund auditing is wanted, a follow-up additive migration can widen the CHECK to include `refund`. (`services/agent/src/sparkpoint_agent/api/quota.py`, `db/limits.py`, tests)

### Changed / Fixed (A9 UX quick fixes — 061)

- **What's New no longer blocks the app.** It is a dismissible corner panel rather than a full-screen modal with a scrim, and "once per release" is keyed to the newest changelog entry's slug in localStorage (so it survives re-seeds that renumber rows). The stale 2026-10-06 broker-mint-whitelist seed entry is pruned by seed ingest (`services/agent/content_seed/whats-new.json`).
- **`GET /brokers/owned` answers instead of 400ing.** A missing or unsupported chain identity now returns `200 {available:false, reason:"no-account"}` (auth unchanged; unreadable chain still says `chain-unreadable`, never an empty list), and the browser sends `X-Chain-Id` on the normal path.
- **No recommendation 404s.** The web only asks for recommendations once a mandate exists (the agent correctly 404s without one), and a race/404 is treated as an empty state.
- **`/vault` stops polling forever.** Vault reads poll every 60s (not every few seconds), never in a hidden tab, back off exponentially on failure, and stop after five consecutive failures; a manual refetch still works.
- **Sign-in no longer hangs on a failed onboarding probe.** `/verify` used to sit on "Checking your access…" forever when the `/mandate` read failed (e.g. the agent answered 404 for a wallet with no plan), contradicting its own fail-open comment. The failed probe now settles as "unknown/not onboarded", so the page reaches "You're in." and offers the pre-025 start/skip CTAs.
- **Strategies is hidden when there is nothing to rank.** The desktop and mobile primary navs drop it only on a known-empty result; it stays deep-linkable and in ⌘K.
- **Phone header and trust banner.** At phone widths the header keeps the mark, notifications and account (address/network/self-custody/stage collapse into the existing menus), and the trust banner can be dismissed permanently.
- **Chat slow state, BFF idle deadline and retry.** With no first content after ~8s the waiting row says "Sparky is taking longer than usual…"; the BFF chat proxy now uses an **idle/stall** deadline — 60s without any upstream chunk (including SSE keepalive/comment frames), re-armed on every chunk, so a genuinely slow but live answer is not cut off — plus a separate 5-minute absolute stream ceiling as a backstop. On a real stall it closes with the same clean `error` block; the existing Retry resends the message. The post-timeout drain is bounded too, and the agent turn is still not cancelled by the BFF, so agent refund semantics mean a failed turn does not double-charge. All of it (response-headers/connect deadline, idle deadline, ceiling) is scoped to the exact `/chat` stream path, so every other proxied agent endpoint behaves exactly as before; the idle timer also runs only while a pull is awaiting the upstream, so a consumer applying backpressure can never manufacture a false timeout.
- **Rate-limit/error copy is no longer a dead end.** It says when the allowance resets and points at Vault, Swap, Portfolio and `/changelog` — all of which work without Sparky.
- **Thumbs feedback now persists.** Finished live answers offer up/down, POSTed to `/chat/feedback` with an optional reason. Migration `0043_chat_feedback` stores one row per user+message (idempotent upsert, last write wins), client-supplied ids are length-capped, and the endpoint is capped at 60 taps per wallet per hour (429; the tap stays non-fatal in the UI). The sink is fail-open.


### Improved (chat latency — A2 streaming + A3 single-call, 059)

- **Sparky's prose now streams to the browser as it is generated.** The LLM client opens the LiteLLM gateway with `stream=true` for the final prose responders (`Sparky`, `Literacy`) and the agent frames incremental `event: text-delta` records (`{target, delta, streamId}`) ahead of the unchanged final `text` / `tip` block. The final block still carries the complete, post-processed answer, is the only prose persisted, and replaces the streamed paint — so guardrail discards, `MAX_CHARS` clamps, retries and fallbacks win exactly as before. A retry emits a new `streamId`, so the client resets instead of concatenating attempts. Deltas are additive: a client or proxy that ignores the event sees the pre-059 behaviour. A mid-stream failure keeps 054/056 semantics — one refund, one `UPSTREAM_UNAVAILABLE` error block, no partial answer persisted. (`llm/client.py`, `agents/sparky.py`, `agents/literacy.py`, `coordinator/dag.py`, `api/chat.py`, `apps/web/src/lib/agent/client.ts`, `apps/web/src/lib/chat/streamText.ts`, chat renderer)
- **Obvious read-only turns no longer pay for a separate planner call.** `SPARKY_SINGLE_CALL` adds a narrow pre-classifier (portfolio / concept question / greeting) AFTER every 057 deterministic route, so a portfolio read makes **0** model calls (was 1), and a concept or conversational answer makes **1** (was 2). Explicit commands, swaps, refusals, guidance and proposals are untouched and still reach the planner. (`coordinator/dag.py`)
- **Flags:** `SPARKY_STREAMING` and `SPARKY_SINGLE_CALL` default **ON** (a fresh beta deploy gets the faster path); set either to `0`/`false`/`off` to restore the exact pre-059 path. Neither flag can affect the deterministic policy gate or signing. (`config.py`, `api/chat.py`)
- **The single-call shortcut yields to ANY action signal, on every fast route.** A compound message like "How do vaults work? I want to deposit 500 USDC" now stays on the planner path instead of being answered only as a concept tip. The same conservative pre-check gates the portfolio, concept and greeting shortcuts, so a signal can only cost one extra planner call — never silently drop the action half of a request. The action guard now covers the full command vocabulary (put, move, invest, stake/unstake, lend, borrow, repay, allocate, transfer, send, bridge, buy/sell, swap, trade, deposit, withdraw, redeem, claim, exit, close, open, convert, rebalance, park, add, remove, pull, cash out, purchase, and their inflections) and treats **any** standalone number/percentage (`100`, `12.5`, `1k`, `2.5m`, `5%`) or written-out amount ("five hundred", "half", "all of my") as blocking unless it is clearly a known token/standard/time window (`ERC-4626`, `ERC20`, `L2`, `24h`, `7d APY`, `30-day`). Blocking only runs the normal planner. Follow-up turns are also protected: with conversation history, anaphora/deictic references ("how does it work?", "what about fees?", "and the risks?") and very short follow-ups skip the shortcut instead of becoming a decontextualised `topic="it"` tip, and a pronoun/stopword can never be a LEARN topic. Streamed prose also holds its opening (~40 chars) until it passes the same action/meta guard as the final block, so an attempt that is already unusable in its opening is never painted; a body that only becomes unusable after the hold may have been painted and is then replaced by the authoritative final block. (`coordinator/dag.py`, `agents/guard_text.py`, `agents/sparky.py`, `agents/literacy.py`)
- **Fast routes are ALLOWLIST-based, not blocklist-based (defence-in-depth).** The zero-/single-call shortcut now fires only on fully anchored shapes after normalising case/punctuation/whitespace and polite filler ("please", "can you", "hey sparky"): explicit portfolio snapshots ("what's in my wallet", "show (me) my portfolio/holdings/balance(s)", "what do I hold/own/have", "my portfolio", "portfolio", "balance", "how much USDC/ETH do I have") and tightly anchored concept asks ("what is/what's/what are/explain/define …", "how does X work") with a short topic phrase and no extra clause. Anything else — advice ("should I", "how should I", "what happens if", "can I", "why"), hypotheticals, compounds, conjunctions, action verbs or numbers — goes to the planner. "How should I fund my wallet?" and "What happens if I lose my wallet?" no longer get a zero-call snapshot, and the blocking guard remains as a second layer, now also covering fund, lose/lost, contribute, top up, recover, fix, help me and should. Table-driven tests pin 38 negatives and the 0-/1-call budget for every positive. (`coordinator/dag.py`, `tests/coordinator/test_streaming_and_single_call.py`)
- **The provisional streamed text no longer double-announces for screen readers.** The live paint is `aria-live="off"` + `aria-busy="true"`; only the authoritative final block is a polite live region, so the answer is announced once when it lands rather than once per chunk and again on replacement. (`apps/web/src/components/screens/chat/live-blocks.tsx`)
- **BFF:** the existing SSE passthrough already sets `Content-Type: text/event-stream`, `Cache-Control: no-cache, no-transform`, `X-Accel-Buffering: no` and `Content-Encoding: identity`; documented and left unchanged. (`apps/web/src/app/api/agent/[...path]/route.ts`)
