3 · Why the permissions are slow (this is the feature, not the bug)

Permission 1 — setDeployer

Allows the scoped AI deployer key (0xB555…D875) to call createVault on the factory. Nothing else — no upgrade, no allowlist, no pause.

HUMAN Safe A (3-of-5) signs a schedule tx → 72h timelock → anyone executes.

Permission 2 — setAllowlisted(child)

Tells the factory that this specific child vault may be wrapped. Even with the deployer role, the factory refuses any child not on this list.

HUMAN Safe A signs again → another 72h → execute.

The 72 hours is enforced on-chain by the TimelockController, not by a calendar reminder. During the window, the independent 2-of-3 canceller Safe can veto. That is why we can’t just “flip it on” — and why nobody (including us, including the AI) can rush a wrapper out.
