# Try the multi-chain beta

The beta is live at https://beta.sparkpoint.io (production Coolify app from the main branch; there is no separate develop web environment). Sign in with a wallet at /connect, then use the manual /swap, /vault and /portfolio pages or ask Sparky to show the same multi-chain picture in chat. Live earn is off by default: you can preview and review, and when an owner enables a flag you sign every transaction in your own wallet. SparkPoint never holds your funds.
