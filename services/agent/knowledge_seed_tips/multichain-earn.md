# Earn on three chains, one page

The Vault page shows the same earn registry Sparky reads: Morpho on Base and Ethereum, Kamino on Solana, and Rhea on NEAR. Filter by chain, preview a vault in Sparky, and see honestly when a rail needs a funded wallet. Live deposit and withdraw reviews are off by default; when an owner enables a flag you review an unsigned transaction and sign it in your own wallet. Nothing signs or sends from the board.
